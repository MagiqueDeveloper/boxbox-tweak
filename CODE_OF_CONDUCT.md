# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds this project up:

- Being kind to people who are new to Objective-C, to the runtime, or to iOS
  tooling generally.
- Giving and accepting specific, technical feedback — "this hook will fire on the
  wrong presenter because…" rather than "this is wrong".
- Assuming good faith, and asking a question before deciding someone is being
  careless.
- Respecting that maintainers work on this in their spare time.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, or deliberately derailing discussion.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Encouraging others to use this project to attack, defraud, or extract payment
  from anyone.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and
whenever an individual is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer
through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** tab, or by
contacting `@MagiqueDeveloper` directly. Reports are reviewed and handled
confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code and issues that do not follow this
code, and will explain the reason when they do. Consequences scale with
severity and repetition, from a private warning up to a permanent ban from the
project.
